About Ekasi Eatery

Who we are

Ekasi Eatery is a local food business that provides delicious, affordable and freshly prepared meals to our community.

Our eatery was created to give people a place where they can enjoy good food, friendly service and a welcoming atmosphere.

Our mission

Our mission is to provide tasty, affordable and quality meals while giving our customers excellent service.

Our vision

Our vision is to become one of the most popular and trusted local eateries in our community.

Why choose us?

  • Affordable prices
  • Friendly customer service
  • A variety of meal options
  • A comfortable and welcoming environment
